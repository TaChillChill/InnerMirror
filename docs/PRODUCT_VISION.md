# Product Vision

## One line
InnerMirror helps people see themselves more clearly through their everyday conversations.

## Problem
After a tense conversation, people often replay *what the other person said* but rarely notice *how they themselves shifted*: from calm problem-solving to defending, scolding, or pleasing. Without that awareness, the same patterns repeat.

## Approach
Use Transactional Analysis (TA), inspired by Eric Berne's *Games People Play* (VI: *Trò Chơi Tâm Lý*), as a friendly vocabulary for those shifts. The AI suggests possible ego states per message, shows where the conversation turned, then hands control to the user.

## Objectives
- **Primary:** help users understand their own reactions, communication patterns, and possible motivations.
- **Secondary:** help users explore how they might respond differently next time.

## Philosophy
- Awareness before change.
- The AI offers interpretations; the user is the authority on their own experience.
- Curiosity over shame, judgment, or overconfidence in AI.

## What it is not
Not a diagnostic tool, not a relationship judge, not therapy, not a profile of the other person.

## Audience
Adults curious about their communication patterns. Use cases: romance, family, friendship, work, everyday misunderstandings.
Hackathon focus: adult-to-adult text chats from screenshots, 2+ participants.

## Success criteria
- **Primary:** after one reflection, the user can say "I understand more clearly why I may have reacted that way."
- **Secondary:** "Next time, I might choose to respond differently."
- **Demo-level proxy (hackathon):** a first-time viewer understands the full loop in a 2–3 minute video, and a test user completes the flow end to end without help.

## Development principle
Build the smallest experience that creates a meaningful moment of self-awareness. Correctness over complexity, user agency over AI authority, clarity over jargon, privacy over data collection, one complete journey over disconnected features.
